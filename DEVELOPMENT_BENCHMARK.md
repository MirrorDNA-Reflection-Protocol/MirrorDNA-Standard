### Best Practices Identified

#### 1. Strategic Phase Planning
- Break work into logical phases
- Complete one phase before starting next
- Maintain clear phase boundaries
- Document phase completion

- Clear, concise requirements
- Trust the system's capabilities

- Full documentation as you go
